Tips for high quality

Start from the original photo, not a screenshot. Use JPG for forms. Set a realistic KB limit (for example 50–100 KB for 300×400). Do not enlarge a small photo. Use Fit or Manual so the face is never cropped.

Common questions

Why does the quality look lower?

Resizing and compressing always lowers quality a little, in every tool. Official sites and apps accept it, and nobody zooms into your photo on the web.

It will not go under my KB limit

Use JPG or WebP (PNG quality cannot be lowered), or make the width and height smaller.

Which format for forms?

JPG is accepted almost everywhere. Use PNG only when you need a transparent background.

What size do admission applications ask for?

It differs by university and form. Photos are commonly around 300×300 or 300×400 pixels and signatures around 300×80 pixels, often with a 20–100 KB limit. Always follow the notice of your university or portal.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, on your device.
